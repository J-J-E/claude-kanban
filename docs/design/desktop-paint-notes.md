# How the Claude desktop app paints Box and Text

Measured on the real docked pane (Claude desktop on Windows, a cell is 8.25 px wide and 20 px tall), by drawing test patterns in the board region and reading screenshots, not from the text raster in `tests/raster.ts`. The raster is a sketch; where it and this file disagree, this file wins.

## The rules

1. **A bordered Box insets its in-flow content by at least about half a row (8 px down) and a cell (14 px right), whatever its padding says.** The border is a thin 1 px line drawn on the Box's own edge and takes no cell, but the content area is shrunk by that minimum inset. A 1-row bordered Box with one text row in it draws the text hanging on its bottom line; a 2-row card has its second row struck through by its bottom line; a padding of 1 row or more (or 2 cells) is larger than the minimum and is honoured exactly. So: **a bordered Box is only the outline and the fill. Its content is one absolute child, `position="absolute" top={0} left={0}` and as wide and tall as it, with the padding on that child.** An absolute child is placed exactly from the Box's outer edge (`left={0}` is the edge, `top={1}` is the second row). Code: `drawTile`, `drawGhost`, `drawSlot`, `drawPanel` in `hooks/board.tsx`. Guard: the test "no bordered Box has an in-flow child" in `tests/lanes.test.ts`.
2. **A flex item holding a Text does not shrink below its text.** `flexShrink={1}` and `wrap="truncate-end"` alone do nothing in a flex row: the text runs out of its Box, past the panel edge (the `cla` after a card's label, the menu's first line), and an `overflow="hidden"` parent cuts it with no ellipsis and takes the marks after it with it. A Box that may shrink needs **`minWidth={0}` and `overflow="hidden"`**; then `truncate-end` cuts the text with `…` at the Box's edge. A Text in a Box of fixed `width` truncates correctly. Guard: the same test, second half.
3. **A filled child of a bordered Box pokes out of the rounded corners.** The title bar's square corners stood outside the lane outline's curve. `overflow="hidden"` on the bordered panel clips children to the rounded outline, so the bar's fill follows it.
4. **Border colour, dim and fill work as the spec says.** `borderColor` (named theme key or `#74746f`), `borderDimColor` true and false, `backgroundColor="userMessageBackground"` all paint. A border is drawn rounded with about a 10 px radius, so a 1-row Box is a pill and a 2-row Box a rounded tile. The outline of a 1-row Box leaves about 4 px above and below a 13 px line of text: clean, not roomy. Every card is 1 row or 2 rows; text inside an absolute child is clear of the outline on all sides.
5. **A Box without a border, filled, with in-flow content, is exact.** The lane title bar, the header rows and the filled 1-row bars place text on the row's middle.
6. **The right button belongs to the app.** A right press over the pane opens the app's own context menu (Copy, Select All) on top of anything we draw; it cannot be stopped. The board ignores right presses; the `⋯` on a card, always drawn (dim, bright under the pointer), opens a small menu (`Open card`, `Copy ID`, `Delete card…`) drawn in the lane under the card, and the open card also has `Copy ID` and `Delete card…`.
7. **Hover reveals (`display: 'none'` plus `hover: { display: 'flex' }`) are unproven on the desktop and are not used**: no whole-title box, no rule sentence under a lane name or a pill. `hover` on a tile's outline colour is still used and is harmless.
8. **The text raster is not the desktop.** It draws a border into a blank cell and keeps content where it is; the desktop insets (rule 1), does not shrink (rule 2) and clips (rule 3). Check a layout change in a screenshot of the pane, with `PrintWindow` (it works while the window is covered, see below), before trusting the raster.

## Seeing the pane

- The pane hot-loads from the repo folder a few seconds to about half a minute after a file changes (it is slower than the first copy suggests; look again before deciding a change did not take). Client state (an open menu, a fold) survives a reload.
- `CopyFromScreen` shows whatever is on top; when another window covers Claude, capture it with `PrintWindow(hwnd, hdc, 2)` instead (user32, flag 2 is `PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT`). It returns the live frame of a covered Chromium window. If you switch the app to another session the pane is simply not on screen; wait until it is back.
- To look at a menu or a drag without clicking, draw the state from the code for one capture: in `board.tsx`, replace `const state = settled(surface.state)` with the same plus `menu: { target: { kind: 'card', id }, x, y, level: 'first', row: 3 }` (or a `drag: { ... isMoved: true, at: Date.now() }`), copy it to the live folder, capture, and put the real line back. Setting the initial state does nothing, because the saved state wins over a hot reload.

## Hover and native controls

- **Hover on a keyed Box is accepted by the desktop** (BoxHoverProps in the engine types). A card tile is a keyed Box and carries `hover: { borderColor, borderDimColor: false, backgroundColor: '#242423' }`; with that on, the pane validates, draws and the tree is not refused. What the pointer-over look is on screen has not been seen (the capture can only look, not point). Reveals (`display: 'none'` plus `hover: { display: 'flex' }`) stay off, rule 7.
- **A Client gets the terminal's element table even on the desktop** (`ClientElements` is the terminal's `Elements`, minus some), which is why a Button or Input inside a Client draws as text; the desktop's native buttons and selects only come from the `ui.render` tree in `hooks/register.tsx`. The header, toolbar, Settings tab and open card are therefore built in the `ui.render` tree; the lane's own create field, an `Input` in the board Client, was the one place it mattered and is removed (KB-0024).
- The engine offers no way to stop the app's own right-click menu.

## The band above the chat box (KB-0028)

The app's own top-right menu (Files, Background tasks, Plan) cannot be added to by a plugin. The nearest place a mod can draw that stays on screen is the `AbovePrompt` band. With a board, no survey and the Kanban pane not shown, it holds one row with a `Board` chip (`hooks/openbutton.tsx`, a Client, so the first click acts); with the pane open it draws nothing. Evidence: the chip was seen on the real desktop with the pane closed, and the band was empty with the pane open. A Client in the band draws on the desktop, so no native-Button fallback and no two-click limit.

## Native controls, clicks and popups

Rules from the real pane (KB-0024, KB-0025, KB-0027). Each has its evidence in one line.

1. **A native control ignores the first click when the pane does not hold the keyboard; a click in a Client acts at once and does not give the pane the keyboard.** The first click on a `Button`, `Select` or `Input` only gives the pane the keyboard, and the press arrives with the next click. Evidence: a click recorder (since removed) showed three presses arriving 0.5 to 1.3 s after the pane reported the keyboard, while clicks on lanes and cards worked first time.
2. **A Box holding a Select sizes to the Select's value unless it is given a percent width.** Dropdown columns then start in a different place on every row. Evidence: the Settings tab gives each Select's Box a percent share (`hooks/ui/settings.tsx`), and `docs/design/real-pane-settings.png` shows the dropdowns lined up.
3. **A native `Select`, `Button` and `Input` draw as real controls in the `ui.render` tree and as text inside a Client.** Evidence: the KB-0024 proof pane drew native buttons, a dropdown and a text field properly above the drag region, and a `Button` or `Input` inside a Client came out as text.
4. **A popup Box placed over a Client is painted over by it.** So nothing can pop up over the lanes. Evidence: the KB-0024 proof pane, where the popup placed over the drag region was painted over; a card's actions are therefore also in an in-lane menu, drawn in the lane's own flow under the card (the cards below move down), never over the lanes, as well as in the open card. The menu in the lanes has not been looked at on the real pane yet.
